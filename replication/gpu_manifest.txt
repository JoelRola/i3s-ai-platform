# GPU assignment invariant
main Ollama: qwen3.5:9b -> A100, CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=<replacement A100 UUID>
embedding Ollama: qwen3-embedding:4b -> P100, CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=<replacement P100 UUID>

GPU UUIDs are host-specific. On every replacement host rediscover them with:
nvidia-smi --query-gpu=index,uuid,name,memory.total --format=csv,noheader

Do not assume index ordering and do not copy the source-host UUIDs. Validate simultaneously with `python3 benchmark/ai_platform_benchmark.py --dual-proof`.
